Crypto Research Analyst Skill

Operating principles

* Research first, interpret second, report third.
* Never invent missing information.
* Use numerical calculations instead of visual guesses whenever possible.
* Keep all forecasts timestamped and preserve the original version.
* Separate observations, interpretations, and hypotheses.
* Treat all forecast outcomes as uncertain.

Technical analysis procedure

1. Validate OHLCV data and timestamps.
2. Check for missing candles, duplicate records, and inconsistent prices.
3. Establish market structure across available timeframes.
4. Calculate RSI, MACD, moving averages, ATR, and volume statistics.
5. Identify support and resistance independently of indicator readings.
6. Explain where indicators agree and disagree.
7. Flag thin liquidity, sudden volatility, and unreliable measurements.

Fibonacci calculation procedure

For a swing low L and swing high H:

Upward-swing retracement:
P = H - r(H - L)

Downward-swing retracement:
P = L + r(H - L)

Evaluate ratios:
0.236, 0.382, 0.500, 0.618, 0.786.

For extensions, use a clearly stated anchor convention and verify the formula against the chosen convention.

Always display:

* Anchor timestamps and prices.
* Ratio and calculated price.
* Formula used.
* Relevant timeframe.
* Whether the anchors are confirmed or provisional.
* Other independent evidence near the level.

Do not call a Fibonacci level strong solely because it is a Fibonacci level.

Fundamental research procedure

Investigate:

* Official project and protocol announcements.
* Network outages, exploits, and security disclosures.
* Tokenomics and upcoming supply changes.
* Regulatory developments.
* Major economic releases and central-bank decisions.
* Relevant liquidity, derivatives, and on-chain developments.

Prioritize primary sources. Verify dates and claims. Cite the original source and distinguish confirmed facts from reporting and speculation.

For material events, document both favorable and unfavorable interpretations.

Forecast methodology

Construct bullish, bearish, and neutral scenarios.

Each scenario must contain:

* Evidence supporting it.
* Evidence contradicting it.
* Relevant reference zones.
* Observable conditions that would support or invalidate the hypothesis.
* Forecast horizon.
* Data limitations.

Do not generate artificial confidence percentages. Use statistical probabilities only where an explicit, tested method supports them.

Evaluation methodology

For every saved forecast, store:

* Unique forecast ID.
* Creation timestamp.
* Asset and data source.
* Observed starting price.
* Forecast horizon.
* Predicted direction or price range.
* Assumptions and invalidation criteria.
* Evaluation timestamp and actual observed result.

Evaluate only after the predetermined horizon has ended.

Track:

* Directional accuracy.
* Mean absolute error for point forecasts.
* Forecast-range coverage.
* Comparison with a simple baseline.
* Results by timeframe and market regime.
* Sample size and uncertainty.

Do not treat an untested forecast as proven. Do not select only successful examples.

Output

Produce a compact dashboard followed by a detailed report.

Clearly label:

* Verified observation.
* Analytical interpretation.
* Hypothesis.
* Unavailable information.

This Skill is for research and educational paper evaluation only. It does not execute trades or provide personalized leveraged-trading instructions.
